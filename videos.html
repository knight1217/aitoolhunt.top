🎯 What Kind of Video Are You Making?

1. Social Media Shorts

Pika for creative AI clips, CapCut for TikTok-ready edits, Opus Clip to repurpose long content into shorts.

2. AI Presenter Videos

HeyGen or Synthesia for professional avatar-led videos. Perfect for training, demos, and explainer content.

3. Professional Editing

Descript for text-based editing, Runway for advanced AI effects. Ideal for podcasts, YouTube, and client work.

4. Experimental & Creative

Sora and Runway Gen-3 push boundaries. Generate entirely new footage from imagination — no camera, no actors.
