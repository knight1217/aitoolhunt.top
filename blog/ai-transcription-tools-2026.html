Best AI Transcription Tools 2026 (Meetings, Podcasts, Videos)

AI transcription has gone from "pretty good" to "near-perfect" in 2026. Here are the best tools for every use case — from meeting notes to podcast transcripts.

Taking manual notes during meetings is a waste of time. Podcast transcripts used to cost hundreds of dollars per episode. In 2026, AI transcription is fast, accurate, and affordable. Here's what works best.

Best for Meetings

1. Fireflies.ai — AI Note-Taker

Fireflies joins your Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams calls automatically. It transcribes everything, generates summaries, and extracts action items. Search across all your meeting transcripts by keyword. Free tier: 800 min storage. Pro: $10/month unlimited.

2. Otter.ai — Real-Time Transcription

Otter provides real-time transcripts during meetings with speaker identification. The AI generates summaries, outlines, and action items automatically. Integrates with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. Free: 300 min/month. Pro: $16.99/month.

Best for Content Creators

3. Descript — Transcript-Based Video Editor

Descript transcribes video and lets you edit by editing the transcript text. Cut words to trim video, remove filler words with one click, and generate captions. The AI can even clone your voice to fix audio mistakes. Hobbyist: free. Creator: $24/month.

4. Riverside.fm — Studio-Quality Recording + AI Transcripts

Riverside records in studio quality (48kHz WAV) and provides AI-generated transcripts, show notes, and social media clips. Ideal for podcasters who want professional audio + AI workflow. Free tier, Pro from $19/month.

Best for Developers & DIY

5. OpenAI Whisper — Free, Local Transcription

Whisper is OpenAI's open-source speech recognition model. Run it locally (free, private, no limits) or via API ($0.006/min). Handles 99 languages, punctuation, and timestamps. The large-v3 model is near-human accuracy. Best for developers and privacy-conscious users.

6. Notta — Fast, Affordable Web App

Notta transcribes audio/video files or live meetings with real-time bilingual support (English + 40+ languages). Clean web interface, export to TXT/DOCX/SRT. Free tier: 120 min/month. Pro: $13.99/month for 1,800 minutes.

Quick Picks

  • For meetings: Fireflies or Otter — both excellent, Fireflies is cheaper
  • For video creators: Descript — transcript-based editing is a game-changer
  • For podcasters: Riverside — best audio quality + AI workflow
  • For developers: Whisper — free, private, and highly accurate
  • Budget pick: CapCut — free auto-captions for social media videos
