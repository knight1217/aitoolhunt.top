How to Create AI Images: Complete Beginner's Guide 2026

Step-by-step tutorial for absolute beginners. Learn how to generate AI images, write effective prompts, choose the right tool, and avoid common mistakes.

AI image generation feels like magic — type words, get a picture. But getting good results takes more than random prompts. This guide walks you through everything from choosing a tool to mastering advanced techniques.

Step 1: Choose Your AI Image Tool

Different tools excel at different things. Pick based on what you want to create:

Midjourney — Best Overall Quality

The gold standard for AI art. Photorealistic, artistic, cinematic — Midjourney does it all. Runs through Discord. $10/month for basic plan. Best for: professionals, artists, anyone who needs top quality.

Leonardo AI — Best Free Option

Free tier gives 150 images/day with excellent quality. Built-in fine-tuned models for different styles. Web-based, no Discord needed. Best for: beginners, experimenting, budget-conscious creators.

DALL-E 3 (ChatGPT) — Best for Beginners

Built into ChatGPT, DALL-E 3 understands natural language better than any other tool. Just describe what you want conversationally. Best for: absolute beginners, quick generations.

Adobe Firefly — Best for Commercial Use

Trained on licensed content, so you can legally use outputs commercially. Integrated with Photoshop and Express. Best for: designers, businesses, anyone selling their work.

Step 2: Master Prompt Writing

A good prompt has four elements — the more specific, the better the result:

  • Subject: What is in the image? (e.g., 'a woman reading a book')
  • Style: What art style? (e.g., 'watercolor painting', 'photorealistic', 'anime')
  • Details: Context, lighting, colors (e.g., 'warm sunset light, cozy cafe')
  • Parameters: Aspect ratio, quality settings (e.g., '--ar 16:9')

Prompt Examples — Bad vs Good

❌ Bad: 'a dog'

Vague. You'll get a generic dog in generic lighting.

✅ Good: 'a golden retriever puppy sitting in a sunlit meadow, photorealistic, shallow depth of field, golden hour lighting, 8K'

Specific subject, style, lighting, and quality. Much better results.

Step 3: Iterate and Refine

Your first generation is rarely perfect. AI image tools support iteration:

  • Variations: Generate similar versions of a result you like
  • Inpainting: Select part of an image and regenerate just that area
  • Upscaling: Increase resolution without losing quality
  • Remix: Keep the composition but change the style

Step 4: Avoid Common Mistakes

  • Too many subjects: Keep it to 1-2 main subjects for clean results
  • Contradicting styles: 'Photorealistic cartoon' confuses the AI — pick one
  • Ignoring aspect ratio: Social media needs 1:1 or 9:16, wallpapers need 16:9
  • Giving up after one try: Generate 4-8 variations before judging a prompt
