AI Interior Design Tools 2026

Upload a photo of any room and AI redesigns it in seconds. The best tools for room makeovers, furniture visualization, and style exploration — no design experience needed.

Seeing Before You Build

Interior design's biggest challenge has always been visualization — you can't truly know how a room will look until you change it. AI interior design tools solve this by generating photorealistic room transformations from a single photo. Upload your living room, pick a style (minimalist, boho, mid-century modern, industrial), and see it reimagined in 30 seconds.

Here are the best tools for different needs and budgets.

AI Room Redesign & Style Transfer

Interior AI — Best All-Around Room Transformer

Price: Free (watermarked). Pro from $19/month.

Interior AI is the most popular AI room redesign tool — and for good reason. Upload a photo, select from 20+ design styles, and get 4 photorealistic redesigns in seconds. The results in 2026 are remarkably coherent: furniture placement makes sense, lighting is natural, and structural elements (windows, doors) stay in the right places. Pro plan includes high-res downloads and "virtual staging" for empty rooms.

Best for: Homeowners exploring renovation ideas. The free tier is generous enough to test 3-4 styles per room before committing to Pro.

Remodeled.ai — Advanced Control & Precision

Price: From $12/month.

Remodeled.ai offers more control than Interior AI — you can specify which elements to keep and which to change. Want new flooring but keep your existing couch? Draw a mask around what stays. The AI also generates shopping links for furniture and decor items that match the generated design, making it useful beyond inspiration.

Furniture Placement & 3D Room Planning

Planner 5D — AI + Floor Plan Design

Price: Free basic. Premium from $6.99/month.

Planner 5D combines traditional floor plan design with AI-powered furnishing. Draw your room dimensions, place walls, doors, and windows, then let AI furnish the entire space based on your chosen style. Switch between 2D floor plan, 3D walkthrough, and AI-generated realistic renders. The furniture catalog includes 5,000+ real items from IKEA, West Elm, and other retailers.

Standout feature: "Design Generator" — describe a room in words and it creates a complete 3D design with furniture placement.

RoomGPT — Fastest AI Room Generator

Price: Free (limited). Pro from $9/month.

RoomGPT gained viral popularity for its simplicity: upload, wait 10 seconds, get a redesigned room. It supports 8 themes (Modern, Minimalist, Professional, Tropical, Vintage, Industrial, Neoclassic, and more). While it lacks the fine control of other tools, the speed and quality are excellent for quick inspiration. Good for testing whether you even like a style direction.

AI for Real Estate & Virtual Staging

Virtual Staging AI — Furnish Empty Listings

Price: From $15/photo.

Real estate agents use this to virtually furnish empty properties — turning cold, vacant rooms into warm, styled spaces in minutes. Upload an empty room photo, select a style, and the AI adds sofas, beds, tables, rugs, and decor with realistic lighting and shadows. Properties listed with virtual staging sell 75% faster according to industry data.

Spacely AI — Architecture-Focused Rendering

Price: From $25/month.

Spacely AI targets architects and interior designers with higher-quality, more controllable renders. It respects architectural constraints better than general tools — ceiling heights, window proportions, and structural elements stay accurate. Good for professionals who need client-ready visuals rather than casual inspiration.

The Bottom Line

AI interior design tools have crossed the quality threshold where they're genuinely useful for decisions — not just fun to play with. For homeowners, Interior AI and RoomGPT are the best starting points (free tiers available). For anyone doing actual renovation planning, Planner 5D with its AI Design Generator offers the best blend of precision and inspiration. Real estate professionals should subscribe to a dedicated virtual staging tool like Virtual Staging AI — it pays for itself with faster sales.
