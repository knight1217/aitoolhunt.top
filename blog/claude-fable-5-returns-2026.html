Claude Fable 5 Returns: #1 Coding AI Is Back

After export controls lifted, Claude Fable 5 is back with a vengeance — 80.3% on SWE-bench Pro and the undisputed coding champion. Here's what you need to know.

On July 1, 2026, Anthropic restored Claude Fable 5 globally after the US Department of Commerce lifted export control restrictions on June 30. The model had been unavailable for months outside select regions, but now it's back — and its benchmark numbers are staggering. Fable 5 reclaims the #1 position in AI coding with a score that makes every other model look ordinary.

Benchmarks That Set the Standard

Fable 5's numbers speak for themselves. On SWE-bench Pro, the gold standard for real-world coding tasks, it scores 80.3% — the highest ever recorded. On SWE-bench Verified, it hits 95.0%, meaning it correctly solves virtually every verified coding problem it encounters. These aren't toy benchmarks; SWE-bench tests on real GitHub issues from popular open-source projects. Fable 5 can fix bugs, implement features, and refactor codebases that would challenge a senior engineer.

Pricing: What Fable 5 Costs

Fable 5 is priced at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens, available through the Max plan at $100/month. This puts it firmly in the premium tier — roughly 5x the cost of Claude Sonnet 5 and 2x the cost of Opus 4.8. For context, a typical coding session with heavy back-and-forth might burn through 500K input and 100K output tokens, costing around $10 in API fees. Is it worth it? That depends entirely on what you're building.

The Claude Family: Which Model When?

Anthropic now offers a clear three-tier lineup, and knowing which to use will save you serious money.

Claude Sonnet 5 — Best Value ($2/$10 per 1M tokens)

The workhorse. Sonnet 5 beats GPT-5.5 on most coding benchmarks and handles 90% of daily development tasks at a fraction of Fable 5's cost. For writing CRUD endpoints, fixing routine bugs, and generating boilerplate, Sonnet 5 is all you need.

Claude Opus 4.8 — The Practical Default ($5/$25 per 1M tokens)

The daily driver. Opus 4.8 sits in the sweet spot — notably better than Sonnet 5 on complex reasoning tasks but half the price of Fable 5. For most professional teams, this is the pragmatic choice. You get near-Fable intelligence on architectural decisions without the premium pricing.

Claude Fable 5 — The Ceiling ($10/$50 per 1M tokens, Max plan $100/mo)

The specialist. Use Fable 5 when the task demands the highest possible ceiling: long-horizon agentic coding sessions that span hundreds of steps, autonomous refactoring of complex legacy codebases, and multi-file architecture decisions where one mistake cascades. This is the model you reach for when Sonnet or Opus isn't enough.

When Fable 5 Is Worth It

Fable 5 shines in three specific scenarios. Long-horizon agentic coding — when you kick off an autonomous agent that will work for 30+ minutes across dozens of files, the higher reasoning ceiling prevents compounding errors. Autonomous refactoring — restructuring a messy codebase without human supervision requires the highest reliability. Complex architecture decisions — when you need to evaluate tradeoffs across multiple system components and design a coherent solution, Fable 5's depth makes a real difference.

For everything else — daily PRs, bug fixes, unit tests, documentation — Sonnet 5 or Opus 4.8 will serve you just as well at a much lower cost. The gap between Fable and Opus on a single bug fix is negligible; the gap only widens on tasks measured in hours, not minutes.

The Verdict

Claude Fable 5 is the unquestioned king of AI coding — but it's a specialist tool, not a daily driver. For most teams, the practical recommendation is Opus 4.8 at $5/$25 as the default, with Fable 5 reserved for the 5-10% of tasks that genuinely need the highest ceiling. If you're on a budget, Sonnet 5 at $2/$10 still outperforms everything else in its price class. The Claude family now gives you a model for every budget — which one you pick just depends on how much ceiling you're willing to pay for.
