What Is Veo 3.1? Google's AI Video Generator Explained

Veo 3.1 is now the undisputed #1 AI video generator. Here's what it can do, how to access it, and whether it's worth your time.

When OpenAI retired the Sora 2 consumer app on April 26, 2026, the AI video throne opened up — and Google DeepMind's Veo 3.1 stepped in without hesitation. Built on years of video generation research, Veo 3.1 delivers the best physics consistency, native audio, and 1080p output of any model on the market. If you're looking for the best AI video tool in July 2026, this is the one to beat.

What Makes Veo 3.1 Special?

Veo 3.1 is Google DeepMind's latest video generation model, and it brings three features that set it apart. First, native audio generation — Veo doesn't just create silent clips; it generates synchronized sound effects, ambient audio, and even music alongside the video. No other major model does this out of the box. Second, its physics consistency is unmatched. Water flows naturally, objects fall with realistic weight, and characters move without the jittery artifacts that plague competitors. Third, it outputs genuine 1080p video with sharp detail, not upscaled lower-resolution footage.

How to Access Veo 3.1

Google has made Veo 3.1 surprisingly accessible. There are three entry points:

Gemini App — The easiest way. Veo 3.1 is integrated into the Gemini app on both web and mobile. Free tier users get limited generations, while Gemini Advanced subscribers get expanded access.

Google AI Studio — For developers who want API access and fine-grained control over parameters like duration, aspect ratio, and prompt weighting.

Vertex AI — The enterprise option with team management, higher rate limits, and SLAs.

Best Use Cases

Veo 3.1 excels where realism matters most. Cinematic scenes with complex camera movement, nature and landscape videos with flowing water or drifting clouds, and any shot where physics-heavy action (objects falling, liquids pouring, fabric moving) needs to look believable. For social media content, the fast generation speed keeps it competitive. Where it's less ideal: highly stylized animation or projects requiring frame-by-frame creative control — that's still Runway Gen-4's domain.

Pricing

Veo 3.1 is included with Gemini Advanced at $20/month (via Google One), giving you a generous monthly quota of video generations alongside all other Gemini features. For developers, Google AI Studio offers pay-as-you-go API pricing — competitive with other video generation APIs but with the added value of native audio. Enterprise pricing through Vertex AI scales with usage.

Veo 3.1 vs the Competition

How does Veo 3.1 stack up against alternatives?

Veo 3.1 vs Runway Gen-4

Runway Gen-4 offers more creative control — Motion Brush, camera direction tools, and 4K output — making it the choice for professional filmmakers who want to craft every shot. Veo wins on convenience, native audio, and physics realism. If you're a filmmaker, Runway. If you want the best auto-generated video, Veo.

Veo 3.1 vs Kling 3.5

Kling 3.5 is faster and cheaper, with strong results for short social clips. But it lacks native audio and falls behind on complex physics scenes. For budget-conscious creators churning out TikTok content, Kling is a solid pick. For quality, Veo is the clear winner.

The Verdict

Veo 3.1 is the best pure AI video generator available in July 2026. Native audio, unmatched physics, 1080p output, and broad accessibility through the Gemini app make it the default choice for quality-first creators. If you're just getting started with AI video or looking for the best all-around tool, start with Veo 3.1 in Gemini — it's free to try and the results speak for themselves.
