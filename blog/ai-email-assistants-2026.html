Best AI Email Assistants 2026

Draft emails in seconds, summarize long threads instantly, and reach inbox zero with AI. Compare the best AI email tools that actually save you hours every week.

Why You Need an AI Email Assistant

The average professional spends 28% of their workweek on email — roughly 11 hours. AI email assistants can cut that in half by drafting replies, summarizing long threads, prioritizing important messages, and even scheduling follow-ups automatically. The best tools aren't just faster; they make your communication clearer and more effective.

Full-Featured AI Email Clients

Superhuman AI — The Speed King

Price: $30/month.

Superhuman built the fastest email client on the market, then layered AI on top. Its AI features include instant reply drafting that matches your voice, one-click thread summarization, and "Ask AI" — where you can query your inbox like a database ("Show me all emails from clients about the Q2 budget that I haven't replied to"). Keyboard shortcuts for everything including AI actions.

Standout: Superhuman AI learns your writing style from previous emails and mimics it in drafts — recipients often can't tell the difference. Best for executives and anyone handling 100+ emails daily.

Shortwave — Smartest AI Inbox

Price: Free tier. Premium from $12/month.

Shortwave organizes your inbox with AI bundles — automatically grouping related emails by sender, topic, or project. Its AI assistant drafts replies, summarizes threads, and translates messages. The "deliver later" feature uses AI to determine when you're likely to read an email and surfaces it at the right time. Built by ex-Google engineers.

Best for: Gmail users who want AI inbox organization without switching workflows. The free tier is genuinely useful.

Missive — Team Email + AI Collaboration

Price: Free (limited). Pro from $18/user/month.

Missive combines email, chat, and task management in one app — ideal for teams handling shared inboxes (support@, sales@). Its AI drafts responses, translates between 50+ languages, and suggests actions. Team members can comment on emails internally before sending, and AI can be triggered by @mention in team chats.

Best for: Teams managing shared inboxes. The combination of internal chat + AI drafting makes it uniquely powerful for customer-facing teams.

AI Email Writing & Summarization Add-Ons

Grammarly — AI Writing Assistant for Email

Price: Free basic. Premium $12/month. Business $15/member/month.

Grammarly's AI assistant integrates directly into Gmail, Outlook, and most web email clients. Beyond spellcheck and grammar, it now offers tone adjustment ("make this more confident" or "more diplomatic"), full email drafting from bullet points, and one-click summarization. Works across your entire browser, not just email.

Flowrite — AI Email Templates

Price: From $15/month.

Flowrite specializes in common email scenarios: introductions, follow-ups, meeting requests, and status updates. You describe what you want in a few words ("follow up with the client about the delayed shipment"), and Flowrite generates a complete, professional email. Great for people who write the same types of emails repeatedly and want to eliminate the friction.

Sanebox — AI Filtering & Prioritization

Price: From $7/month.

Sanebox doesn't write emails — it makes sure you only see the ones that matter. Its AI analyzes your email behavior and automatically sorts incoming messages into folders: important, newsletters, bulk, and cold emails. It also offers one-click unsubscribe from newsletters and AI-powered reminders for emails that need follow-up. No AI drafting, but the best at inbox triage.

Two Minutes to Better Email

If you want the simplest possible upgrade: install Grammarly's free browser extension (works everywhere, improves every email) and try Shortwave's free tier (replaces Gmail's web interface with AI organization). Together, they'll save you 3-5 hours per week for $0.

For power users: Superhuman is the gold standard if email is a core part of your job. The $30/month feels steep until you realize it's saving you 10+ hours a month — that's $3/hour for time saved.
