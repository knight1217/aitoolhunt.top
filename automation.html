⚙️ What Can You Automate with AI?

1. Data Entry & Transfer

Move data between apps automatically. New email → CRM entry. Form submission → spreadsheet. Use Browse AI for web scraping and Fireflies for meeting notes.

2. Web Monitoring & Scraping

Track competitor prices, monitor job listings, get stock alerts. Browse AI watches websites 24/7 and notifies you of changes.

3. AI-Powered Workflows

Add AI decision-making to your automations. Mem and Taskade help manage projects with AI-powered organization and task generation.

4. Calendar & Scheduling

Let AI manage your time. Motion, Reclaim.ai, and Clockwise optimize your schedule, protect focus time, and handle rescheduling automatically.
