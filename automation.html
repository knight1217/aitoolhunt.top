⚙️ What Can You Automate with AI?

1. Data Entry & Transfer

Move data between apps automatically. New email → CRM entry. Form submission → spreadsheet. Eliminate copy-paste forever with Zapier or Make.

2. Web Monitoring & Scraping

Track competitor prices, monitor job listings, get stock alerts. Browse AI watches websites 24/7 and notifies you of changes.

3. AI-Powered Workflows

Add AI decision-making to your automations. n8n and Relevance AI let you route tasks, classify data, and generate content inside workflows.

4. Calendar & Scheduling

Let AI manage your time. Motion and Reclaim.ai optimize your schedule, protect focus time, and handle rescheduling automatically.
