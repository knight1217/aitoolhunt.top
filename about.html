About AI Tool Hunt

Why We Built This

Every day, dozens of new AI tools launch. Most of them are garbage. A few are genuinely game-changing. Trying to figure out which is which by yourself is exhausting — and expensive.

We built AI Tool Hunt to cut through the noise. Every tool listed here has been tested, compared, and rated honestly. We tell you what it does well, what it does poorly, and who it's actually for.

How We Choose Tools

We don't list every AI tool. We curate. A tool makes it onto our directory only if:

✅ It actually works as advertised
✅ It solves a real problem
✅ The pricing is transparent (no bait-and-switch)
✅ We've used it ourselves

How We Make Money

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click through and sign up for a tool, we may earn a small commission — at zero extra cost to you. This does not affect our ratings or recommendations. We recommend tools because they're good, not because they pay us.
