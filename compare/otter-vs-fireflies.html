Otter.ai vs Fireflies.ai in 2026: Which AI Meeting Assistant Wins?

Two of the most popular AI meeting transcription tools go head-to-head. We compare accuracy, integrations, pricing, and real-world performance to help you pick the right one.

Quick Verdict

Otter.ai wins for individual professionals who attend lots of meetings and need the most accurate transcription, especially for in-person conversations. Fireflies.ai wins for teams needing broad integrations, CRM sync, and automated meeting analytics. For most teams, Fireflies is the better value.

Winner: Fireflies.ai. Cheaper for teams, more generous free tier, and unlimited transcription at all paid levels. Otter's minute caps can be restrictive for heavy meeting users.

Transcription Accuracy

Otter.ai wins here. Otter's transcription engine consistently scores higher in accuracy tests, especially for:

  • Speaker identification: Otter distinguishes between speakers more reliably, even in overlapping conversations. Fireflies sometimes merges or misattributes speakers.
  • In-person meetings: Otter's mobile app captures live, in-room conversations well. Fireflies is primarily designed for virtual meetings and struggles with in-person audio.
  • Technical terminology: Otter handles industry jargon, acronyms, and technical terms with fewer errors. Fireflies is slightly less accurate with domain-specific language.
  • Accent handling: Otter performs better with non-native English speakers and varied accents.
Real-world test: Recorded a 45-minute strategy meeting with 4 participants on both tools. Otter had 3 minor transcription errors. Fireflies had 7, including 2 misattributed quotes. Both were usable — Otter just needed less manual correction.

Fireflies auto-joins calendar meetings and records without prompting — you set it up once and forget about it. Otter requires more manual intervention and has far fewer integrations. If you use a CRM or collaborate in Slack, Fireflies is the clear choice.

AI Features & Analytics

Fireflies.ai is more feature-rich for team workflows:

  • Super Summaries: Fireflies generates structured meeting notes with action items, key topics, and outline — automatically. Otter's AI summaries are simpler and less actionable.
  • Smart Search: Fireflies lets you search across ALL your meetings for keywords, questions, or action items. Otter's search is per-meeting.
  • Conversation Intelligence: Fireflies tracks metrics like talk-to-listen ratio, filler word count, and sentiment — valuable for sales coaching and team analytics. Otter lacks this entirely.
  • Soundbites: Fireflies lets you clip and share key moments from meetings as short video/audio snippets. Useful for sharing decisions with stakeholders who missed the meeting.
  • Threads & Comments: Fireflies adds a collaboration layer — team members can comment on specific moments in a transcript and create threads. Otter has basic commenting on the full transcript only.

Otter's AI features focus on the individual: auto-generated meeting titles, keyword highlights, and a meeting calendar view. Solid, but narrower in scope.

Ease of Use

Both are easy to set up, but differently:

  • Otter.ai: Install the app, grant calendar access, and Otter joins scheduled meetings. Simple and clean. The mobile app is excellent for recording in-person meetings. The web interface is intuitive with a real-time transcription view that updates as people speak.
  • Fireflies.ai: Connect your calendar, invite the Fireflies bot (fred@fireflies.ai), and it auto-joins. Slightly more setup initially because of the bot invitation step, but zero ongoing management. The dashboard is more complex due to more features, but the core recording experience is seamless.

Winner: Otter for simplicity. Fireflies for "set it and forget it" once configured.
