Notion AI vs Craft AI (2026): The Battle of Smart Documents

Two beautiful writing apps with AI built in — but one is a platform and the other is a focused editor. Which AI-powered tool earns a place in your workflow?

Writing Experience: Craft's Sweet Spot

Craft feels like writing on paper that's smarter than you. Its block-based editor is fluid, beautiful, and responds instantly on native Apple Silicon apps. The AI is woven in unobtrusively — highlight text, hit the AI button, and choose from drafting, editing, translating, or summarizing. There's no friction. The document is the star, and the AI is a quiet assistant.

Notion's writing experience is powerful but heavier. The block system supports everything from simple text to embedded databases, which makes it incredibly flexible, but the UI can feel cluttered. Notion AI lives in every block — you can summon it with `/ai` or Space — which is convenient, but the AI sometimes feels bolted on rather than integrated. Notion excels at structured writing (meeting notes with action items, project specs, database entries) while Craft excels at flow writing (essays, articles, creative work).

Real-world test: "Write a 500-word blog post about AI in healthcare, with a professional but engaging tone"

Craft's AI produced a more engaging, naturally flowing post with better paragraph transitions. Notion AI's draft was more factual and structured, reading like a solid first draft from a junior writer. Craft's output felt more human; Notion's felt like a well-organized outline expanded into prose.

AI-Powered Workspace: Notion's Domain

Where Notion truly dominates is its AI-powered workspace search. You can ask "What did we decide about the Q3 budget in last month's meeting?" and Notion AI scans your entire workspace — meeting notes, project pages, databases, and documents — to find the answer. This is genuinely transformative for teams that live in Notion.

Craft's AI Q&A is limited to the document you're currently in. For teams managing dozens of documents and projects, Notion's cross-workspace AI is a killer feature that Craft can't match. Notion also uses AI to auto-fill database properties, generate summaries of long pages, and create action items from meeting notes — practical AI applications that save real time.

Pricing: Craft Is the Value Play

Notion AI costs $10 per member per month on top of your Notion plan. This adds up fast: a 10-person team on Notion Plus ($10/member) with AI ($10/member) pays $200/month. Craft's Pro plan is $5/month per user with AI included — a 10-person team pays $50/month. The price difference is substantial, and for teams that mainly write and collaborate on documents (rather than managing complex databases), Craft delivers better value.

That said, Notion's platform can replace multiple tools (project management, wiki, note-taking, lightweight CRM), while Craft is purely a document tool. If Notion's platform features let you cancel other subscriptions, the higher price may be justifiable.

Design & Performance: Craft's Native Advantage

Craft's macOS and iOS apps are native — built with Swift and SwiftUI. They launch instantly, scroll at 120fps, and feel like first-party Apple apps. Offline mode actually works. Notion's Electron-based apps are functional but sluggish, and offline mode is notoriously unreliable. If you value speed and polish, Craft is in a different league.

Notion's visual design is more flexible — you can heavily customize layouts with columns, callouts, colors, and embeds. Craft is deliberately opinionated about beautiful typography and spacing, which means everything looks great by default but you have less control. Notion is a canvas; Craft is a magazine layout.

Verdict: Platform vs Editor

Choose Notion AI if you:

  • Need a full workspace (wikis, databases, project management)
  • Want AI to answer questions across your entire knowledge base
  • Manage teams with structured workflows and templates
  • Use database-driven pages (CRM, inventory, sprint planning)
  • Need AI to auto-fill and categorize database entries

Choose Craft AI if you:

  • Primarily write — essays, articles, documentation, notes
  • Value beautiful, fast native apps (especially on Apple devices)
  • Need reliable offline editing
  • Want AI writing help without the workspace complexity
  • Prefer a focused tool that does one thing exceptionally well
  • Are cost-conscious — $5/mo with AI included

🏆 Bottom Line

Notion AI is a Swiss Army knife with a sharp AI blade — it does everything reasonably well and the cross-workspace AI search is genuinely game-changing. Craft AI is a precision writing tool — focused, fast, and beautiful. If you run a team, Notion's the answer. If you're a writer or individual knowledge worker who wants AI-assisted prose in a gorgeous, fast editor, Craft is the hidden gem nobody talks about enough. At $5/month with AI included, it's one of the best values in productivity software.
