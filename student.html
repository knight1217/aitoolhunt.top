📋 How to Pick the Right AI Tool for Your Studies

1. Writing Papers

Use Claude for deep analysis and Grammarly for polish. Run drafts through both for best results.

2. Studying for Exams

Use Quizlet AI to generate flashcards and Khanmigo for concept explanations. Spaced repetition beats cramming.

3. Research Projects

Use Perplexity to find sources and Consensus for peer-reviewed evidence. Always verify AI citations yourself.

4. Group Projects

Use Notion AI for shared notes and Canva AI for group presentations. Keep everything in one workspace.
