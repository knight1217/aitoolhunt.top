What is Clockwise?

Clockwise is an AI-powered calendar assistant that optimizes your schedule to maximize deep work time. It connects to Google Calendar, analyzes everyone's schedules, and automatically moves flexible meetings to create blocks of uninterrupted focus time. The 2026 version includes 'Focus Time Protection' that prevents new meeting invites from fragmenting your deep work blocks. Team analytics show meeting load and focus time trends.

✅ Pros

  • ✔ Creates uninterrupted focus blocks automatically
  • ✔ Teams: meeting optimization across group
  • ✔ Focus Time Protection in 2026
  • ✔ Free for individuals

❌ Cons

  • ✘ Requires Google Calendar (no Outlook)
  • ✘ Only works if meetings are reschedulable
  • ✘ Can feel disruptive to meeting routines
  • ✘ Teams plan adds per-user cost

📖 How to Use Clockwise

  1. Sign up at getclockwise.com and connect Google Calendar.
  2. Clockwise analyzes your meeting patterns.
  3. Set your focus time preferences (when and how much).
  4. Clockwise automatically moves flexible meetings to optimize your schedule.
  5. Use 'Schedule Send' to batch meeting bookings into optimal slots.
  6. View team analytics to see meeting load and focus time.
  7. Set 'Quiet Hours' to prevent scheduling during personal time.
