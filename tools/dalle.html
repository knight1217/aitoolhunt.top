What is DALL-E 3?

DALL-E 3 excels at understanding detailed text descriptions and rendering them accurately. Integrated directly into ChatGPT, you can refine images through conversation. Great for precise, text-heavy images and complex scene compositions.

📖 How to Use DALL-E 3

  1. Subscribe to ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) at chat.openai.com.
  2. Start a chat with GPT-4o and describe the image you want.
  3. Be specific: 'Generate an image of a cozy bookstore cafe, warm lighting, someone reading by the window, watercolor style'.
  4. If the result isn't right, say 'Make it brighter' or 'Remove the cat' — ChatGPT refines iteratively.
  5. DALL-E 3 handles text in images better than Midjourney: try 'A poster that says WELCOME in bold letters'.
  6. Images are 1024×1024 by default; ask for 'widescreen 1792×1024' for landscape. Download images with right-click → Save.
