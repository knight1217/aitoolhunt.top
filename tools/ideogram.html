What is Ideogram?

Ideogram cracked the hardest problem in AI image generation: rendering readable text within images. It excels at logos, posters, t-shirt designs, and memes where text accuracy matters. The V2 model produces stunning images with precise typography. Web app is clean and beginner-friendly.

📖 How to Use Ideogram

  1. Go to ideogram.ai and sign up with Google (free: 20 slow generations/day).
  2. Type your prompt — include text in quotes: 'A vintage travel poster saying EXPLORE in bold gold letters, art deco style'.
  3. Choose aspect ratio: square (1:1), landscape (16:9), portrait (10:16).
  4. Pick a style: Realistic, Design, 3D, Anime, etc.
  5. For logos: 'Minimalist tech logo, the word NEXUS in sleek sans-serif, blue and purple gradient, clean background'.
  6. Remix feature: click any public image → Remix → modify the prompt to create variations. Plus plan ($8/mo) gives 400 fast generations + private mode.
