# aitoolhunt.top 推广方案 V4.0

**定位：英文AI工具导航站 | 受众：全球英文用户**
**原则：只发综合性大平台（像知乎那种什么人都有的大杂烩），不碰任何AI垂直社区**

## 一、综合问答平台（海外版知乎）

### 1. Quora
- 网址：https://www.quora.com
- 做法：搜索 "best AI tools for..." 相关问题，写高质量回答，自然引出你的网站

**发帖文案：**
> **Question: What are the best free AI tools for content creation in 2026?**
>
> I've tested dozens of AI tools this year. Here are my top picks that are actually free:
>
> 1. **ChatGPT (free tier)** — Still the best all-rounder for writing, brainstorming, coding help
> 2. **Claude (free tier)** — Better at long-form writing and analysis
> 3. **Leonardo AI (free tier)** — Great for image generation without Midjourney's price
> 4. **Gamma** — Free AI presentation builder, surprisingly good
>
> I recently built a directory at **aitoolhunt.top** where I organize these by category (writing, image, video, code, etc.) with pricing and feature comparisons. It's free, no signup needed — just a clean list if you want to explore more options.
>
> Hope this helps anyone drowning in AI tool choices!

## 九、不要做的事

- ❌ 不要发AI垂直社区（Reddit AI子版块、AI Newsletter、AI Discord）
- ❌ 不要发AI导航站（那是你的竞争对手）
- ❌ 不要发Product Hunt（太卷，纯AI工具海，你的优势不明显）
- ❌ 不要付费推广（现阶段不值得）
- ❌ 不要机械复制粘贴 — 每个平台的文案要根据平台调性调整
